| Token | Hex | Use |

| mint | #E6F6EE | pills, icon tiles |

## Look

"Glass & signal", taken from the SuDu company-profile cover: frosted glass tiles over a soft mint glow, with ✦ sparkles on tile corners. Neon is used as one signal per slide (a highlight bar under the key phrase, a glowing dot), never as body text on light.
