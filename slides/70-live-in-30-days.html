Onboarding

3 steps to start using your AI

1
STEP 1

AI setup

SQL Integration

The SuDu sync tool connects SQL Account safely. Customers, items, prices, stock and credit limits sync on their own, and nothing writes straight into your database.

  • Connect SQL Account
  • Sync customers, items and prices
  • Set permissions and approvers
2
STEP 2

Train the AI

Skill / SOP · Item Code

Like teaching a new staff member: write your way of working as Skills and SOPs. Then teach it your customers’ words: which item code in your system is “bwg besar”.

  • Write down Skills and SOPs
  • Match item codes
  • Test with last month’s orders
3
STEP 3

Start using your AI

Go Live

The AI takes orders, chases payments, checks stock and makes reports. At first it only drafts, and nothing is saved until you approve. Once you trust it, add jobs 1 at a time.

  • Drafts first, you approve
  • Every action is logged
  • Add 1 job at a time
