WHY IT HAPPENS

Manual Picking

Pick lists written by hand. Wrong item, wrong quantity, and nobody updates the system.

No fixed shelf spot

Goods are left wherever there is space. Nobody records which shelf.

Stock cannot be found

It is in the warehouse, but nobody knows where. Staff spend hours searching.

Oldest stock not sent first

New cartons go out first. The old ones sit at the back and expire.

UOM mixed up

10 pieces keyed as 10 cartons. The numbers get more wrong every day.

Typed in the next day

Goods move today, the system knows tomorrow. In between, every figure is wrong.
