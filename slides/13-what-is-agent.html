PART 1 OF 5

Brain

It understands the message and plans the next step.

EXAMPLE

“bwg besar 10kg x ---” → big onions, 10 kg bags. Quantity missing, so it asks.

PART 2 OF 5 NO TOOLS, NO WORK

Tools

Each tool is 1 action in a real system. The brain cannot click; tools can.

EXAMPLE

Check stock → 42 bags. Create order → draft ready.

PART 3 OF 5

Memory

The brain forgets after every chat, so it looks up your records.

EXAMPLE

Jaya Grocer’s last price for big onions: RM28 a bag.

PART 4 OF 5

Rules & skills

Your way of working, written down: what never to do, and the steps.

EXAMPLE

Over the credit limit? Stop and ask the boss.

PART 5 OF 5 KEEPS IT SAFE

Permission

What it may see and do. Anything important waits for a person.

EXAMPLE

It can draft an order. It cannot delete an invoice.
