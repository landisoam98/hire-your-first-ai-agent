Chatting with your data vs running your company

A connector + Claude or ChatGPT
SuDu AI
WhatsApp
No groups. It knows a customer only if their phone matches SQL Account.
Reads group and private chats by itself, even from unsaved numbers. Matches customer and item nicknames.
Orders
Staff key in orders 1 at a time. Customer self-service is a paid add-on.
Customer orders on WhatsApp turn into Sales Order drafts. You just approve.
Follow-up
A scheduled message fires once and is done. No waiting for replies.
Tasks that run for days: send, wait for the reply, follow up, stop when paid.
Memory
Reads SQL Account live for each question.
A memory layer: every customer’s history, habits and past chats, across millions of records.
If it goes down
1 Windows PC, always on. Everything on 1 disk: no backup, nobody alerted.
The cloud: monitored, with alerts. Big questions run on SuDu’s data platform, not your SQL Account server.
Setup & access
On-site IT. No mobile app; admin page only on that PC. Licence tied to 1 PC.
Sign in on web or mobile. Several companies in 1 account.

SQL Account runs on a Firebird database, built for daily bookkeeping, not for an AI scanning years of data. Heavy AI questions on it can clash with your staff’s work.
