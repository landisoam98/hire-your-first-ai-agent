1950s–80s

Rules

THINK OF IT LIKE

Like a recipe book. The computer follows every step exactly.

People write every rule by hand: if this, then that.

WELL-KNOWN
Excel formulasCredit-limit checkExpert systems
IN YOUR COMPANY

Block an order over the credit limit

2000s

Machine learning

THINK OF IT LIKE

Like a clerk who has seen 10,000 invoices and just knows who pays late.

Show it past data. It finds the pattern by itself.

WELL-KNOWN
Spam filterCredit score“You may also like”
IN YOUR COMPANY

Predict who will pay late

Needs neat tables. 1 model answers 1 question.

2012

Deep learning

THINK OF IT LIKE

Like a child who learns what a cat is by seeing 1,000 cats.

Many layers learn from millions of photos and sounds.

WELL-KNOWN
Face unlockSiriGoogle Translate
IN YOUR COMPANY

Read a handwritten order or a bank slip

Each model does only 1 job.

2018–20

LLM

Like your phone’s autocomplete, but it read the whole internet.

It predicts the next word, again and again.

WELL-KNOWN
ChatGPTClaudeGeminiDeepSeek
